# Paired Growth Audit

**Live → https://eddieatadapty.github.io/paired-flow-rebuild/**

Five ideas to turn more Paired users into subscribers, each built into a clickable version of Paired's own onboarding. Prepared by Adapty.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Say their names back before the price | +10–20% CR to purchase |
| 02 | Ask for notifications after the paywall, not before | +10–15% CR to purchase |
| 03 | Let them answer one question before they pay | +5–15% CR to purchase |
| 04 | Put your 74% and your 4.7★ where people decide | +5–10% CR to purchase |
| 05 | Offer a year that doesn’t renew to people who close | +5–10% ARPU |

Every price, rating and feature comes from Paired's App Store listing, website or in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty’s expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across 30,000+ subscription apps, not measured lift for Paired. They overlap, so they don’t add up.

Static single page, no build step: `python3 -m http.server`.
